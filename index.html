Common irregular verbs

These occur constantly in everyday Portuguese and are worth recognising early.

VerbMeaning / use
serto be — identity, origin and inherent characteristics
estarto be — state, condition and location
terto have
irto go — also used with an infinitive for the common future construction
fazerto do / make
podercan / to be able to
quererto want

Irregular verbs have forms that do not always follow the standard -ar, -er or -ir patterns.
